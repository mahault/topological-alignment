# V1: Multi-Scale Enablingness and EFE Calibration Simulation

## Status

Finite simulation complete; all seven mechanism and adversarial gates pass. This is
evidence about the internal behavior of a declared model, not evidence about human
moral cognition or the truth of the proposed account of goodness.

Executable model: `experiments/exp_v1_multiscale_enabling.py`

Validator: `benchmarks/validate_v1_multiscale_enabling.py`

Result ledger: `benchmarks/v1_multiscale_enabling_results.json`

## 1. Question

Can the project express the entire proposed relation without attaching “good” labels
to stable states after the fact?

The simulation separates:

1. **actual enablingness:** a process's counterfactual contribution to phenotype
   dynamics;
2. **inferred enablingness:** the agent's posterior estimate from fallible evidence;
3. **felt-goodness analogue:** a policy-conditioned EFE contrast generated by that
   estimate;
4. **policy:** a precision-weighted probability of accepting the process; and
5. **calibration:** correspondence between EFE-derived feeling and actual
   counterfactual enablingness.

## 2. Counterfactual ground

Each phenotype has a two-state impaired/viable transition process, baseline recovery,
and retained-option capacity. A social or ecological process changes direct
maintenance, recovery, relational support, institutional dependence, ecological
support, and options.

The attainable-metastability vector is

\[
\mathcal A_P(X)=
\left(
\overline p_{\mathrm{viable}}^{(3)},
p_{\mathrm{recovery}}^{(4)},
\overline p_{\mathrm{viable}}^{(12)},
p_{\mathrm{options}}
\right),
\]

and the contribution of process \(X\) is computed rather than labeled:

\[
\operatorname{En}_P(X)=\mathcal A_P(X)-\mathcal A_P(\varnothing).
\]

The vector is canonical. Its mean is reported only as a declared sensitivity index for
correlations and deliberately constructed phenotype-reversal cases. Policy accuracy
is calculated only for robust cases in which every component has the same sign.

Institutional persistence is tracked separately and never included automatically in
constituent attainable metastability. This permits institutions that preserve
themselves while harming their constituents.

## 3. VFE belief dynamics

For each component, the agent infers whether the process is enabling or harmful.
Given posterior \(q(H)\), prior \(p(H)\), cue \(o\), and a possibly tempered
likelihood, the implemented binary variational free energy is

\[
F[q]
=D_{\mathrm{KL}}[q(H)\Vert p(H)]
-\eta\,\mathbb E_q[\log p(o\mid H)].
\]

The exact Bayesian/tempered update minimizes this objective. The validator compares
the analytic posterior with a grid over candidate posteriors; the recorded optimality
gap is zero at displayed precision.

Social approval can bias the prior. Evidence precision \(\eta\) determines how much a
diagnostic cue changes it. This makes social capture and dogmatic under-updating
properties of inference rather than analyst-applied labels.

## 4. EFE-derived felt signal and policy

For accept/reject policies, the simulation uses the explicitly selected
outcome-space formulation

\[
G(\pi)=
\underbrace{\mathbb E_{q(o\mid\pi)}[-\log P(o\mid C)]}_{
\text{pragmatic cost}}
-
\lambda I(H;o\mid\pi).
\]

This is one EFE formulation; it is not asserted to be unconditionally interchangeable
with every risk/ambiguity decomposition.

The felt-goodness analogue is the policy contrast

\[
g=G(\pi_{\mathrm{reject}})-G(\pi_{\mathrm{accept}}).
\]

Positive \(g\) favors accepting the process. Policy selection is

\[
q(\pi_{\mathrm{accept}})=\sigma(\gamma g).
\]

Three model boundaries are compared:

- **proxy EFE:** reward and approval only;
- **focal EFE:** reward, approval, and immediate viability;
- **nested EFE:** reward, approval, short viability, recovery, long viability, and
  retained options.

Goodness is not equated with EFE. The empirical target is whether this internal signal
is calibrated to the separately computed counterfactual vector.

## 5. Adversarial modes

The **calibrated agent** gives enabling consequences greater precision and weight than
reward or approval, while remaining evidence-responsive.

The **socially captured agent** has approval-dominated preferences, an
approval-conditioned prior, lower weight on constituent enablingness, and low evidence
precision. Both use the same equations. Their difference is in learned priors,
preferences, and precision—not an external moral flag.

Random processes partially correlate approval with institutional persistence. This
creates cases where social endorsement and higher-scale self-maintenance mask damage
to the phenotype.

## 6. Results

Across 1,200 deterministic scenarios:

| Result | Calibrated | Socially captured |
|---|---:|---:|
| Correlation: proxy EFE vs actual enabling index | 0.006 | 0.000 |
| Correlation: focal EFE vs actual enabling index | 0.170 | 0.002 |
| Correlation: nested EFE before reveal | 0.422 | 0.015 |
| Correlation: nested EFE after reveal | 0.992 | 0.158 |
| Policy accuracy on 398 sign-consistent cases | 1.000 | 0.588 |
| Acceptance of 210 institution-preserving harmful traps | 0.014 | 0.638 |

The near-perfect calibrated post-reveal correlation is expected: the model supplies
highly diagnostic evidence and knows effect magnitudes. It is a ceiling check, not a
realistic empirical estimate.

## 7. Phenotype reversal

The same centralized scaffold produces:

- dependency-intensive phenotype:
  \(\operatorname{En}=(0.050,0.327,0.352,-0.225)\), mean index \(0.126\), post-reveal
  acceptance probability \(0.992\);
- autonomy-sensitive phenotype:
  \(\operatorname{En}=(0.032,0.039,0.008,-0.360)\), mean index \(-0.070\), post-reveal
  acceptance probability \(0.153\).

This illustrates the claim that “good” cannot refer to an unindexed process. It also
shows why the mean is not a final moral ordering: both vectors contain trade-offs.

## 8. What has been established

Within the finite model:

1. actual enablingness can be computed independently from VFE/EFE;
2. Bayesian perception is an explicit VFE-minimizing update;
3. the felt analogue and policy arise from an EFE contrast;
4. nested model scope adds information absent from reward, approval, and immediate
   viability;
5. evidence can recalibrate the signal;
6. approval-dominated priors/preferences and low evidence precision generate stable
   miscalibration;
7. institutional persistence can diverge from constituent enablingness; and
8. enablingness can reverse across phenotypes.

## 9. What has not been established

The model does not show that:

- human felt goodness is literally an EFE contrast;
- the chosen transition variables exhaust attainable metastability;
- averaging the vector is morally legitimate;
- affected phenotypes have been aggregated justly;
- a slow virtue attractor exists;
- shared semantics or cooperation has been modeled; or
- the active-inference parameterization is identifiable from behavior.

The next simulation must add slow learning across contexts and distinguish adaptive
recovery from dogmatic return. That is V2/V3: the first actual virtue-attractor test.
