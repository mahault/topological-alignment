# Definition Decisions and Virtue Mapping

## Purpose

This document records why the framework uses its current definitions, how each
definition maps onto an operational quantity, and what the V2/V3 ablations permit us
to say about virtue. It is the prose companion to the public claim-by-claim guide.

## Decision procedure

A candidate definition is retained only when it:

1. preserves the philosophical role of the construct;
2. keeps the object being estimated independent of the estimate;
3. maps to variables generated by the model rather than post-hoc labels;
4. distinguishes known counterexamples;
5. supports an identifiable intervention or held-out prediction;
6. separates formal, computational, empirical, and normative proof obligations; and
7. is narrowed when ablation or adversarial evidence does not identify its proposed
   mechanism.

## Canonical definitions and rejected shortcuts

| Construct | Rejected shortcut | Current definition | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goodness | reward, approval, pleasure, or persistence | counterfactual contribution to phenotype-relative attainable metastability | the target must be independent of the feeling and preferences being calibrated |
| Felt goodness | goodness itself | fallible embodied estimate of expected enablingness | feelings can guide action while being mistaken |
| Meaning | lexical similarity or consequences alone | context-indexed profile of expected consequences, policies, affordances, affect, cue mappings, beliefs, and precision | virtue concepts matter through practical organization, not words alone |
| Virtue | any stable trait or attractor | selectively stable calibration of meaning to goodness | dogmatism can also be stable and shared |
| Shared semantics | identical beliefs or correlated actions | reciprocal processability of heterogeneous anticipatory profiles on relevant overlaps | common causes create correlation, while different representations may remain mutually intelligible |
| Cooperation | a cooperate action, mutual information, or one scalar joint EFE | vector of joint attainment, processability, readability, complementarity, causal contribution, and per-agent coupling effects | coordination can be exploitative, coercive, or externally harmful |
| Moral goodness | whatever minimizes VFE or EFE | functional goodness plus an explicit bridge concerning standing, symmetry, floors, non-domination, contestability, repair, and conflict | inference does not determine who counts or which trade-offs are legitimate |

## Operational mapping

The modelled loop is

$$
\operatorname{En}_{P,t}
\longrightarrow g_t
\longrightarrow M_i(V,c)_t
\longrightarrow q_i(\pi_t)
\longrightarrow o_{t+1}
\longrightarrow (g_{t+1},M_{i,t+1},r_{i,t+1}).
$$

| Philosophical role | Model object | Operational interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| phenotype or form of life | $P=(K_P,A_P,N_P,T_P,D_P)$ | viability bounds, capacities, needs, horizons, and dependencies |
| functional goodness | $\operatorname{En}_P$ | counterfactual change under removal or scrambling of an enabling process |
| felt goodness | $g_t$ | EFE contrast or learned prediction of enabling consequences |
| situated construal | $w_t$ | fast context-sensitive semantic weights |
| character or disposition | $r_t$ | slow centre retaining cross-situational learning |
| evidence sensitivity | $\rho_t$ | precision assigned to observed evidence |
| practical relevance | $d_t$ | diagnosticity controlling slow revision |
| context sensitivity | $x(c)\otimes x(\mathrm{support})$ | why the same virtue may reverse its action across contexts |
| explicit attraction candidate | $\kappa\lVert w_t-r_t\rVert^2$ | proposed pull of situated meaning toward slow character |
| action | $q_i(\pi)\propto\exp[-\gamma_iG_i(\pi)]$ | context-sensitive policy posterior under a declared EFE formulation |
| shared meaning | $\operatorname{Proc}_{i\leftrightarrow j}$ | held-out cross-agent translation without identical beliefs |
| cooperative organization | $Q(\pi_1,\pi_2,Y)$ plus interventions | joint consequences and causal contributions without erasing agent-local effects |

The mapping is an operational hypothesis, not an assertion that a parameter is
literally a moral faculty or that historical virtue ethics already contained the
equations.

## Why the mechanism ablations matter

The ablations determine which components currently earn a role in the virtue
interpretation.

| Removed component | Result | Philosophical consequence |
|---|---:|---|
| slow-centre learning | 0/3 environments pass | a virtue-like regime needs retained dispositional learning in this construction |
| evidence-precision gating | 0/3 | selective evidence sensitivity distinguishes stability from both rigidity and volatility |
| context interactions | 0/3 | virtue cannot be represented as one fixed action rule |
| goodness grounding | 0/3 | stability without an independent success condition is not virtue |
| explicit attraction pull | 3/3 | the pull term is not identified as necessary; “attractor” remains a dynamical description |
| EFE policy mapping | 3/3 | EFE is compatible with the signature but not yet uniquely explanatory |

Failure under ablation is only necessity relative to the declared architecture.
Survival under ablation is nevertheless a genuine negative result: the removed term
cannot be presented as necessary for the current signature. No ablation establishes
moral sufficiency.

The current positive mapping is therefore

$$
\underbrace{r_t}_{\text{character}}
+\underbrace{\rho_t d_t}_{\text{practical evidence sensitivity}}
+\underbrace{M(V,c)}_{\text{contextual practical meaning}}
+\underbrace{\operatorname{Cal}(g,\operatorname{En}_P)}_{\text{success condition}}.
$$

This combination is stable without being rigid, adaptable without being fickle,
context-sensitive without being empty, and answerable to a good not defined by the
agent's confidence. V4 and V5 must still determine whether the organization is
jointly enabling and morally admissible.

## Experiment genealogy

| Stage | Setup | Hypothesis earned or tested |
|---|---|---|
| Legacy E-C1–E-C4 | synthetic belief networks, EEG synchrony, semantic clouds, and Fisher/GW geometry | topology may describe dynamical organization; only feasibility survived audit |
| V0 | factorially separate reward, approval, expected consequences, feeling, justification, evidence reveal, and revision | felt goodness may be a separable and fallible heuristic |
| V1 | remove or scramble higher-scale processes for different phenotypes; infer through VFE and select through EFE | the heuristic may be calibrated to phenotype-relative enablingness and systematically captured |
| V2/V3 | compare calibrated, dogmatic, unstable, and opportunistic regimes across noise, recovery, diagnostic change, and washout | virtue may have a selective-stability signature |
| Robustness | 48 parameter configurations across three environments and six gates | the signature occupies a broad sampled region |
| Ablations | remove each proposed mechanism separately | only some components currently earn a virtue interpretation |
| V4 | independently manipulate shared signs, translation, policy coupling, and affected-party outcomes | shared virtue should stabilize jointly enabling meaning, not merely correlated action |
| V5 | matched stable coordination with domination, exploitation, exclusion, and repair | functional success is not sufficient for moral virtue |
| V6 | longitudinal testimony, exemplars, institutions, and local/public learning rates | public concepts and local realizations should co-evolve |
| V7 | held-out adversarial pairs against semantic and ordinary dynamical baselines | topology remains central only if it adds predictive value |
