Where we are: virtue, goodness, meaning, and active inference¶

This notebook is a visual guide to the project as of 2026-07-20. It distinguishes:

  1. the philosophical proposal;
  2. what the simulations actually demonstrate;
  3. what remains untested; and
  4. the next multi-agent experiment.

Current bottom line: we have a robust constructed signature of selective stability: a slow meaning system can resist noisy evidence while revising under diagnostic evidence so that it becomes better calibrated to phenotype-relative enablingness. We have not yet shown that human virtues work this way, that EFE is uniquely necessary, or that the system produces shared moral meaning.

1. The core idea in one picture¶

The crucial distinction is between actual goodness, its felt estimate, and the meaning structure that organizes action. A virtue is hypothesized to be a slow, metastable calibration loop—not merely a stable trait or pleasant feeling.

The vocabulary¶

Construct Operational meaning here Not equivalent to
Goodness Counterfactual contribution to a phenotype's attainable metastability pleasure, reward, approval, persistence
Felt goodness Learned embodied estimate of expected enablingness proof of moral truth
Meaning Context-indexed profile of expected consequences, policies, affordances, affect, and inference word embeddings or verbal definition alone
Shared meaning Heterogeneous meanings that remain reciprocally processable on relevant overlaps identical beliefs or correlated actions
Virtue Selectively stable calibration of meaning to goodness any stable attractor
Cooperation Vector of joint achievement, processability, readability, synergy, causal contribution, and coupling effects action dependence or a cooperate label

3. What the V2/V3 simulation found¶

Four authored regimes were compared. The calibrated regime should combine high ordinary calibration, low movement under noisy evidence, and retained improvement after diagnostic evidence. Dogmatism should recover without learning; instability should move under noise; opportunism should track reward/approval rather than enablingness.

Reading the graph:

  • The calibrated regime is accurate, barely moves under noise, and retains improved policy after diagnostic evidence.
  • The dogmatic regime looks stable, but its transformed-context calibration washes out.
  • The unstable regime learns the transformation, but moves drastically under low-quality noise.
  • The opportunistic regime can remain behaviorally coherent while being almost unrelated to actual enablingness.

This establishes that the diagnostics can distinguish the four programmed dynamics. It does not establish that these are natural psychological kinds.

The honest claim after ablation¶

We can currently claim a robust slow calibration and selective-stability process. We cannot yet claim that the explicit attraction term produces the effect, or that EFE is indispensable. “Attractor” is presently a dynamical description; an EFE-specific experiment must make a prediction that a direct value policy cannot reproduce.

8. What would change our minds?¶

Finding Consequence for the paradigm
Felt goodness does not predict enablingness better than reward/approval Reject or radically revise the goodness heuristic
Meaning measures do not predict action across held-out contexts Reject the proposed semantic-pragmatic operationalization
Shared-sign and processability measures do not distinguish common-cause dependence Reject them as measures of shared semantics
Slow regimes cannot combine noise resistance with diagnostic revision Reject selective stability as the virtue signature
Ordinary dynamical or semantic models outperform topological features Remove topology from the central paradigm
EFE-specific interventions make no predictions beyond simpler control models Treat active inference as compatible language, not explanatory necessity

One-sentence status¶

We have shown that the proposed virtue signature is coherent, discriminable, and robust inside a constructed single-agent model; the next real test is whether it survives a multi-agent world in which shared meaning, cooperation, externalization, and goodness can come apart.
